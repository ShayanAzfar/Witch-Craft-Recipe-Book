# Witch Craft Recipe Book
## Learn new recipe & rituals from the witch by reading her recipe book!

## Desciption
### Its a simple 3d website project and illustration of a witch craft recipe book with multiple pages.
### Made entirely from Vanilla HTML CSS and JS, design the book front and back cover with the help of canva.

## How to Read The Book?
### By clicking on the book page cover, it will open the first page of the book, after that you can continue reading more pages by clicking on the current page, if you want to close the book or start reading from the page 1 you have to click on the back cover of the book.

## Inspiration 
### A witch asked me to publish her book 💀.
